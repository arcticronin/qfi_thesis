%% Abstract (recommended length around 80-200 words; this is not a copy of your thesis assignment!)
\newcommand*{\Abstract}{%
    Quantum Fisher information (QFI) sets the ultimate sensitivity of a parameterized quantum state, but its practical use requires scalable calculations and a model of the complete sensing process.  This thesis studies both needs.  First, it implements the Variational Quantum State Eigensolver within a variational QFI estimation pipeline to obtain truncated and fidelity-based bounds without full density-matrix diagonalization.  Numerical studies of a finite Transverse-Field Ising Model magnetometer show that restricted access can decrease the available QFI and that fixed depolarization lowers it uniformly across the range.  Gibbs preparation can, even considering finite-temperature regimes, outperform reduced ground-state preparation away from its narrow optimum, showing that sensor design must account for the expected operating regime.  Second, the thesis studies high-contrast exoplanet imaging with spatial-mode demultiplexing (SPADE).  Per surviving photon, ideal full SPADE is nearly QFI-optimal for the assumed source model.  However, aggregate first-order counts from a completed ASI Matera experiment cannot support the intended state reconstruction and truncated-QFI analysis.  The experiment is therefore recast as a modular quantum-to-classical channel sequence, together with a predictive classical calibration model.  This framework enables conditional QFI and CFI calculations while showing that the available data constrain only population-level count statistics, not the complete quantum channel or its coherences.
}
