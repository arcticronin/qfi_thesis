Variational spectral estimation
Variational Quantum State Eigensolver (VQSE): a trainable spectral decomposition.

    PCA: covariance matrix                                                VQSE: density matrix
    C = ∑i si vi viT                                                      ρ = ∑i λi |vi ⟩⟨vi |
    Directions ranked by variance.                                        Components ranked by probability weight.



    V (α)ρV † (α) ≈ diag(λ1, λ2, . . .)

    Implemented VQSE supplies spectral information for fidelity-based QFI bounds.
