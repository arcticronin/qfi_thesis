Learning the dominant components of a mixed state
Spectral truncation provides a compact representation for sensitivity estimation.

 We retain the dominant eigencomponents to represent mixed states efficiently.

                Pm
   ρm =             i=1 λi |vi ⟩⟨vi |
                                                                   Retain the m largest eigenvalues
                                                                   and their associated eigenvectors.

 VQSE — Variational Quantum State Eigensolver
 Learns the leading eigenvalues and a circuit that prepares their eigenvectors.
 PCA — Principal Component Analysis: an analogy
 Both retain dominant spectral components: state probabilities here, variances in PCA.
 TQFI — Truncated Quantum Fisher Information
 The retained components provide input to fidelity-based QFI bounds.
VQSE / VQFIE: Cerezo et al. (2022); Beckey et al. (2022). Truncation is subnormalized, not renormalized. Finite-step scope: Appendix A.
