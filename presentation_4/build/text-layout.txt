How we deal with Mixed States
 WHAT             Statistical mixtures of quantum states, described by a density matrix ρ
 WHY              Real sensors are noisy and only partly accessible
                  Mixed-state QFI remains less explored and costly to compute exactly

 HOW              Use truncated spectral decomposition
                                  Pm
                    ρm =             i=1 λi |ψi ⟩⟨ψi |
                                                                                The m largest eigenvalues
                                                                                and their eigenvectors.

                  VQSE — Variational Quantum State Eigensolver
                  Learns the leading eigenvalues and a circuit that prepares their eigenvectors.
                  TQFI — Truncated Quantum Fisher Information
                  Uses the retained components to construct fidelity-based QFI bounds.
